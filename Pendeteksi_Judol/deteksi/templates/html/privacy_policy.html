Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2025

1. Introduction

AntiSlot ("we", "our", or "us") is a research project for academic purposes (Skripsi). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our YouTube Comment Analysis tool.

2. Information We Access

When you choose to log in with Google, we access:

  • Your basic profile information (Name, Avatar).
  • Your YouTube channel permissions (to read and moderate comments on your behalf).

3. How We Use Data

We use this data solely to:

  • Verify your identity.
  • Analyze comments on videos you submit.
  • Perform moderation actions (hide/delete comments) only upon your explicit request.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
